ABOUT BLOCKSMITH

      v0.26 MVP
      
    

    

      

        A single-file, zero-dependency drag & drop HTML studio.

        Build landing pages, portfolios and multi-page sites — then ship clean, self-contained HTML with no build step and no framework.
